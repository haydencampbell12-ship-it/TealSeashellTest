Push notifications

Get a push on your phone for every new booking request and payment. Install the free ntfy app (iPhone or Android), tap +, and subscribe to the same topic name you saved as the NTFY_TOPIC secret. Keep the topic name private: anyone who knows it can read the pushes.

Check-in message

Sent to direct-booking guests the day before check-in (from 9 AM Central), with their name and dates filled in. It's stored privately in your site's database, never on GitHub, so it's safe to include your gate, fitness and Wi-Fi codes. Each guest's unit door code is entered on their booking and fills in {door_code}. Airbnb and VRBO guests don't get it from here: use each platform's own scheduled messages for them.
