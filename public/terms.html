House Rules & Rental Terms

Last updated November 24, 2026

These House Rules and Rental Terms apply to every direct booking at The Teal Seashell, Unit 219 at Fontainebleau Terrace, 14401 Front Beach Rd, Panama City Beach, FL 32413. You agree to them when you pay for your stay. The person who books (the "primary guest") is responsible for everyone in their group.

House rules

  • Guests. No more than 3 people may stay overnight, including children. Only guests named in your booking may stay. Visitors who aren't staying are welcome until 10:00 PM.
  • Age. The primary guest must be at least 25 and must stay in the unit for the whole booking.
  • No parties or events.
  • No smoking or vaping of any kind, anywhere in the unit or on the balcony. This includes marijuana and e-cigarettes. Smoking in the unit means a deep-cleaning charge.
  • No pets. Service animals are welcome. Please let us know before you arrive.
  • Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Fontainebleau Terrace is a condominium with neighbors on every side.
  • Check-in is after 4:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out only with our approval in writing. Your door code stops working at check-out time.
  • Parking. One rental parking pass per unit. Display it as instructed, or your vehicle may be towed at your expense.
  • Wristbands and keys. Wear the resort wristbands at the pool and on the property. There is a $25 charge for each missing wristband, key or parking pass.
  • Building rules. Follow Fontainebleau Terrace's posted rules for the pool, balconies, elevators, grills and common areas. The pool and beach have no lifeguard; swim at your own risk and supervise children at all times.
  • Balconies. Don't hang towels or swimsuits over the railing, and don't throw anything from the balcony.
  • Beach rules. Panama City Beach doesn't allow glass on the beach, and alcohol is banned on the beach during March. Fill in any holes you dig, and take chairs, umbrellas and toys off the beach each evening, because the city removes anything left overnight.
  • No fireworks, candles or open flames in the unit.
  • Take care of the place. Tell us right away if anything breaks or isn't working. Don't move furniture between rooms or take linens, towels or beach gear off the property, except beach gear to the beach.
  • Check-out. Follow the check-out checklist: dishes in the sink, towels in the tub, fridge emptied, trash out, wristbands on the counter.

Booking and payment

Booking requests are reviewed by the owner. When we approve your dates, we email a secure Stripe payment link. Your dates are held for a limited time and your booking is confirmed only once payment goes through. The full amount is due when you book.

Your total includes the nightly rate, a cleaning fee and lodging taxes: Florida sales tax (7%), the Bay County tourist development tax (5%) and the Panama City Beach business tax (1%). Taxes are charged on the lodging and cleaning fee and shown in your quote before you pay.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund.
  • Within 30 days of check-in, your stay is refunded only if we're able to rebook the same nights. If we rebook only some of them, we refund those nights.
  • No refunds for early departures, late arrivals or no-shows.
  • Hurricanes. If a mandatory evacuation order is issued for Panama City Beach that covers your stay, we refund the nights you can't use.
  • We aren't responsible for things outside our control, such as building repairs, pool or elevator closures, utility outages or beach conditions, and these don't qualify for a refund. If the unit becomes unlivable because of something within our control, we'll refund the affected nights.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show them.

Refundable damage hold

When you pay, Stripe securely saves your card. On your check-in day, we place a hold of $250 on that card. A hold is not a charge: it reserves the amount on your card, and nothing is taken unless we have to.

We release the hold 2 days after check-out unless there is damage, missing items, extra cleaning beyond normal use, or a broken house rule (such as smoking or extra guests). In that case we charge only what it costs, up to the amount of the hold, and email you the reason. Whatever we don't charge is released. For damage that costs more than the hold, we'll send you an invoice for the difference with photos and receipts.

Card holds usually last about 7 days, so on longer stays your bank may drop the hold before you check out. You're still responsible for any damage. If the hold can't be placed, we'll ask you for another card.

Conduct and safety

If guests seriously break these rules, such as by throwing a party, having extra people stay overnight, causing a disturbance or damaging the property, we may end the stay early without a refund. You stay at your own risk. We aren't liable for injury, loss or theft of personal belongings, except where the law says we are.

We or our maintenance staff may enter the unit for urgent repairs or if we believe there's an emergency, and will try to let you know first.

Other terms

Your stay is a transient vacation rental. It doesn't create a tenancy or a lease. These terms are governed by the laws of Florida. If any part of them can't be enforced, the rest still applies.
