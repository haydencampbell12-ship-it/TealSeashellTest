About The Teal Seashell

Think a quiet beach in PCB is impossible to find? We’ve got it! Looking for family-friendly things to do? We’ve got that too, at The Teal Seashell at Fontainebleau Terrace!

Go back to a simpler time at the famous Fontainebleau Terrace, rumored to have been a vacation destination for legends like Elvis, Johnny Cash, and Kitty Wells. FBT recently underwent a massive renovation while keeping its iconic retro vibe, and The Teal Seashell has also been updated with a cozy beach-cottage feel.

The Teal Seashell is a private end unit on the 2nd floor with easy elevator access. It has beautiful views of the emerald waters of the Gulf of America and is just steps from the fluffy white sand of private Bid-A-Wee Beach. The unit has everything you need for an amazing beach vacation, including beach chairs and umbrellas, sand toys, and a rolling cooler.

Start your day with morning coffee on the 2nd-floor shared balcony overlooking the water. Maybe you’ll make a new friend from another city! Then head to the beach for a relaxing afternoon, or enjoy some family fun at Pier Park.

After a day of fun in the sun, enjoy savory seafood at one of the many nearby restaurants. If staying in sounds better, cook your own meal on the cooktop. Just bring the groceries, and we’ll provide the rest.

End your day by slipping into cloud-like comfort in one of the only king-size beds at FBT. Soft cooling linens and an extra cooling pillow-top mattress will have you sleeping like a baby and dreaming of your next vacation at The Teal Seashell!

How The Teal Seashell Went from a Dream to Reality

My older sister, Michelle (known as Aunt Chell to my sons and Mrs. Michelle to her kindergarten students), spent many years vacationing on the beaches of the Gulf of America, starting with PCB when we were teenagers. Once we became moms, we dragged our six boys along and lay on the beach watching them “dig to China” and hunt for sea life while we sipped piña coladas and let the days drift by. We used to joke that someday, when we were old and our husbands were gone, we’d still be together, selling straw hats on a Gulf of America beach.

In 2012, to our devastation, my sister was diagnosed with stage 4 ovarian cancer, whose ribbon is teal. After a long, hard five-year battle, my brave big sister and lifelong beach buddy passed away the day after her 50th birthday. In her final moments, I played ocean sounds in her ear as she peacefully went on to a paradise we can only imagine.

This Gulf is the closest thing to Heaven I can imagine. So on July 16, 2026, the anniversary of her passing, I purchased The Teal Seashell. I added teal accents to the décor and named it in her honor. I hope you’ll join me in honoring her memory with a stay at The Teal Seashell, for Michelle.

With love,
Your Host, Renae
