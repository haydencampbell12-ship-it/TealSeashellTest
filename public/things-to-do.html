Things to do

Our favorite places to eat and play in Panama City Beach, starting with the ones you can walk to from Fontainebleau Terrace.

Steps from the condo

Everything here is on Front Beach Road within a short walk of Fontainebleau Terrace, so you can leave the car parked.

Short walk

Hidden Lagoon Super Golf & Racetrack

Practically across the street. Two 18-hole mini-golf courses with waterfalls and a pond full of fish you can feed, plus a go-kart track. Family-owned since 1986.

14414 Front Beach Rd · Mini golf · Go-karts

Directions
Short walk

Runaway Island

Two stories of Gulf-front dining with a big deck and boardwalk out to the sand. Local seafood, frozen drinks and live music. Go for sunset, and book ahead in season.

14521 Front Beach Rd · Seafood · Beachfront

Directions
Short walk

A'More Donuts

An easy walk for coffee, fresh donuts and a quick breakfast before a beach day. Opens early and closes after lunch.

14508 Front Beach Rd · Breakfast · Donuts

Directions
Short walk

Local Steamer Seafood Market

Steamed seafood platters made to order: snow crab, royal red shrimp, corn, potatoes and their house spice. Eat there or carry it back to the condo.

14656 Front Beach Rd · Seafood · Take-out

Order onlineDirections
Short walk

Zollo's Pizza

Order online and walk over to pick it up. Open late, with outdoor tables if you'd rather eat there.

14658 Front Beach Rd · Pizza · Late night

Attractions

Shopping, shows and rainy-day fun along the beach.

Shopping · Dining

Pier Park

Open-air shopping, restaurants and entertainment across the street from the Russell-Fields City Pier. Home to the SkyWheel and a movie theater.

600 Pier Park Dr

Golf · Food & drinks

Topgolf

Two levels of climate-controlled hitting bays next to Pier Park. Clubs are provided, and no golf experience is needed.

15475 Topgolf Way

Views

SkyWheel

A giant Ferris wheel at Pier Park with enclosed, climate-controlled gondolas and views up and down the coast. Pretty at night.

The upside-down building. Over 100 hands-on science exhibits, laser tag and an indoor ropes course. A great rainy-day plan.

Dolly Parton's pirate show near Pier Park: ship battles, acrobats, mermaids and live animals, with a four-course dinner. Buy tickets ahead.

On the water

Dolphins, snorkeling and the white sand of Shell Island.

Beach · Snorkeling · Trails

St. Andrews State Park

Gulf beach on one side and the bay on the other. The calm water inside the jetties is the best easy snorkeling in town. There's a park entry fee.

4607 State Park Ln

WebsiteDirections
Shuttle · Shelling

Shell Island

An undeveloped barrier island you can only reach by boat. The official shuttle leaves from St. Andrews State Park. There's no food, shade or restrooms on the island, so bring water and sunscreen.

St. Andrews State Park

Shuttle infoDirections
Dolphins · Sunset cruises

Dolphin cruises at Capt. Anderson's Marina

The Capt. Anderson III runs Shell Island dolphin trips and sunset cruises from Grand Lagoon. Smaller private dolphin and snorkel boats leave from the same area.

5550 N Lagoon Dr

Kids · Dolphins

Sea Dragon Pirate Cruise

A two-hour cruise on an 85-foot pirate ship. Kids sword-fight, fire the cannon and hunt for treasure, and you'll often spot dolphins.

5325 N Lagoon Dr

Guided trips that combine dolphin watching with a snorkel stop at Shell Island or the jetties. Book a day or two ahead in summer.

Fishing

Cast from a pier or head offshore.

Pier fishing

Russell-Fields City Pier

A 1,500-foot pier across from Pier Park, open 24 hours with a bait shop at the entrance. Buy a daily walk-on or fishing pass. Great at sunrise or sunset even if you don't fish.

16201 Front Beach Rd

WebsiteDirections
Pier fishing

M.B. Miller County Pier

Built as a twin of the City Pier, with its own bait and tackle shop. Spanish mackerel, king mackerel and cobia are caught here in season.

12213 Front Beach Rd

WebsiteDirections
Offshore

Deep-sea and charter fishing

Big party boats for affordable group trips, or private charters for trolling and bottom fishing. Ask when you book whether gear and a fishing license are included.

Capt. Anderson's Marina, 5550 N Lagoon Dr
