Why book here instead of Airbnb or VRBO

Same studio, same calendar. Booking directly means no guest service fee, you talk to the owner from the first message, and you pay through Stripe's secure checkout.

How booking works

What happens after I send a request?

The owner checks the dates against every calendar the studio is listed on and replies, usually within a few hours. If the dates are free, you get an email with a secure payment link. Your dates are confirmed once payment goes through.

Are my dates held while I wait?

Not until your request is approved. After approval the dates are held for 23 hours while you pay. If payment isn't completed, the dates are released.

Why is the calendar sometimes different from Airbnb?

Airbnb and VRBO update shared calendars every few hours. That's why every booking here is reviewed by the owner before you pay.

What's the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 30 days before check-in. Within 30 days, the stay is refundable only if the dates are rebooked.

Why do some nights cost more?

Rates follow the beach season. Summer, spring break and holiday weekends cost more, and Friday and Saturday nights carry a small weekend rate. The calendar shows the price of every night before you pick dates.

Do you offer monthly stays?

Yes. Stays of 28 nights or more starting November through February get the snowbird monthly rate.
